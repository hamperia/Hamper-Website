Help them settle in.

Think beyond the first-day unboxing: a checklist, contact sheet, notebook and welcome message can help a colleague navigate their first week. Avoid printing information that changes frequently.

Use a QR code on your own welcome card for internal resources, and keep sensitive employee or company data out of printed gift packaging.

Personalisation and quantities

Send your preferred colours, message and any logo artwork with the required quantity. The minimum quantity depends on the product and branding method; ask us to confirm it in your quote. Quantity discounts are confirmed individually.

Delivery and corporate orders

Listed-product orders have a flat ₹99 shipping fee per order. Custom and bulk delivery is confirmed in your quotation. Share the delivery postcode, number of addresses and required arrival date so we can check the schedule.

How soon can this order arrive?

Contact us with your postcode, quantity and required date. We confirm production and delivery timing after checking stock and any personalisation; there is no fixed turnaround promised for every order.

What do you need for a corporate quotation?

Include your preferred products, recipient count, budget per gift, logo or message requirements and delivery locations. Ask for the final contents, total charges and delivery plan before confirming.

Can I order online now?

Online payment is being set up. You can save your choices in the basket after signing in, then contact us to confirm an order or request a customised quote.
