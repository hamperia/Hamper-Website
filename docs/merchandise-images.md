# Merchandise catalogue images

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then resized and compressed as WebP for the website. These are illustrative product images, not supplier photographs.

Final assets (relative to the website root):

- `assets/merch-dark-green-tshirt-v2.webp`
- `assets/merch-black-hoodie-v2.webp`
- `assets/merch-cream-mug-v2.webp`
- `assets/merch-black-keychain-v2.webp`

Prompt direction: individual photorealistic ecommerce product photographs on a warm pale cream studio background, soft daylight, entire product visible, generous margins, realistic materials, no collage, text, logos or watermark.

Subjects: dark green cotton crew-neck T-shirt laid flat; black pullover hoodie with hood, drawstrings and kangaroo pocket; cream ceramic mug at a three-quarter angle; black stitched leather keychain with a silver split ring. Hoodie and keychain colours were edited to black using the image generation tool while preserving materials, shape, composition and lighting.

The existing bottle and tumbler photographs are reused. Electronics use the existing square product illustration with square crops, preserving the products' proportions.
