Corporate Diwali gifting: compare contents and plan your order

Choose a festive hamper around your recipients, budget and delivery plan, with a clear list of what each box includes.

By Hamperia Solutions · Practical gifting guide

Start with the recipients

Separate employee and client requirements before choosing a hamper. Check gift policies, dietary needs and whether the gifts will reach one office or individual addresses.

Compare the contents, not only the box

Hamperia’s listed ₹1,499 festive hamper combines almonds, cashews, chocolates, a candle and a message card. The ₹2,499 selection adds a reed diffuser; the ₹3,499 selection also includes decorative Lakshmi and Ganesh idols. Review each product page and confirm quantities, ingredients and presentation before ordering.

Set aside time for personalisation

Prepare your company logo, message and recipient list before requesting a quote. Ask which branding methods are available for your chosen packaging and approve the artwork before production.

Confirm the delivery plan

Share quantity, destination and required arrival date. Ask for the current production and delivery estimate rather than assuming a festive deadline. Confirm the final bulk quotation, shipping and any customisation charges in writing.
