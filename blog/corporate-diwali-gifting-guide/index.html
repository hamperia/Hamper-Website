Corporate Diwali gifts: a practical guide to planning a thoughtful order

Compare festive hamper options, prepare your recipient list and confirm customisation and delivery before the season gets busy.

By Hamperia Solutions · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Plan recipients, quantities and delivery destinations first.
  • Compare the complete contents and food information for each option.
  • Approve the message, branding and written quotation before production.

Start with the people receiving the gift

Make separate lists for employees, clients and business partners if their gift policies, budgets or delivery needs differ. Confirm how many people are included, whether gifts will go to one office or many addresses, and when each group needs to receive them.

For food gifts, ask about ingredient information and common allergens before selecting the assortment. A festive theme can still feel inclusive when the contents are clearly described and the message is warm without making assumptions about how each person celebrates.

Compare contents, presentation and budget

Hamperia’s current Diwali catalogue includes listed hamper options at ₹1,499, ₹2,499 and ₹3,499. The product descriptions show different combinations of dry fruits, chocolates, a candle, a diffuser, decorative idols and a greeting card. Read the live product page for the exact proposed contents and confirm item quantities, food labels and presentation in your quotation.

Compare the complete set rather than the headline price alone. Ask whether the gift box, message card, logo treatment, assembly and delivery are included. If you are considering several price points, make sure each option has a clear contents list so recipients receive what was approved.

Make personalisation easy to approve

Prepare a high-quality logo file, the greeting text and any names that need to appear on individual cards. Decide where branding should appear and whether you prefer a subtle tag, sleeve or printed element. The available method depends on the chosen product and packaging.

Ask for a digital artwork proof and check spelling, colour, logo size and safe margins. Confirm any sample represents the final materials and finish. Keep the approved version with the order so it can be used as the reference during packing.

Build a realistic delivery schedule

Share the quantity, delivery locations and required arrival date when requesting a quote. Ask for the current production estimate and the last date for approving artwork. Multiple destinations, custom branding and different hamper variants can affect the schedule, so confirm a plan for each shipment.

Before final approval, request one written quote that lists contents, quantities, packaging, personalisation, delivery and payment terms. Confirm how substitutions will be handled if a product becomes unavailable. A clear record makes it easier for your team to approve the order and check what arrives.
