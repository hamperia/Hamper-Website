How to make a gift hamper at home

From the first basket to the final ribbon: a practical guide to making a personal gift.

By Hamperia Solutions · Practical gifting guide

Start with one person and one idea

Think about what your recipient enjoys. A tea break, a new job or a birthday can give your hamper a clear direction. Pick a few connected items instead of filling every gap with something unrelated.

Choose the container after the contents

Lay out your items and measure the arrangement before choosing a basket or box. Allow space for protective filler and wrapping. Keep heavier items near the base and avoid putting fragile pieces under pressure.

Arrange, protect and finish

Place taller items at the back and smaller items towards the front. Use filler to support the arrangement, not to hide loose or unstable items. Keep food sealed and separate it from scented products. Test that the contents stay in place before wrapping.

Make it personal

Add a short handwritten note, a name tag or a ribbon in their favourite colour. These details give the gift its meaning. If it will travel, add protective outer packaging and confirm the delivery arrangements.
