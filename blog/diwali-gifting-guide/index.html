Diwali gifting guide: how to choose a hamper for family, employees or clients

Compare Diwali hamper contents, presentation and delivery needs to find a festive gift that suits its recipient.

By Hamperia Solutions · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Choose a gift around the recipient and the relationship.
  • Check food contents, labels, packaging and delivery suitability.
  • For company orders, plan quantities and branding before the festive rush.

Choose the right Diwali gift for the recipient

A Diwali gift for family can be personal and relaxed; an employee gift should feel inclusive across the team; and a client hamper often benefits from a concise note and understated branding. Start with who will receive it, what you want the gift to communicate and whether it will be shared.

Set a budget for each recipient before choosing the assortment. If you are buying for a group, confirm the number of gifts and whether any recipients need a different option. A clear plan makes it easier to compare festive hampers and avoid last-minute changes.

Compare what is inside each hamper

Look at the actual contents and quantities, not only the box or festive artwork. Hamperia’s current Diwali listings show options at ₹1,499, ₹2,499 and ₹3,499 with differing combinations of dry fruits, chocolates, a candle, a diffuser, decorative idols and a greeting card. Read each live product description and confirm the final assortment, jar sizes or quantities, and current price before ordering.

If the gift includes food, request ingredient, allergen, packing-date and storage information. Do not assume a product is suitable for a particular diet unless every item and its handling details have been checked. If an item is unavailable, agree on any replacement and price before it is packed.

Make the presentation festive and practical

A coordinated box, ribbon and greeting card can create a festive first impression. The contents should remain the focus: choose a colour palette that complements the gifts, and leave labels visible where recipients need food or care information.

Think about how the hamper will be carried or delivered. Jars and fragile decorations need protection; candles and fragrance items should be secured and separated from food. Ask how the packaging is prepared for transport to the actual destination.

Plan corporate and bulk Diwali gifting early

For employee and client gifts, prepare the recipient count, delivery list, budget per gift, logo artwork and message before requesting a quote. Ask about branding choices, order quantities, production time and delivery dates. If there are multiple offices or addresses, describe the distribution plan so fulfilment can be estimated accurately.

Review a written quotation with the complete contents, customisation, packing and delivery charges. Approve the message and artwork carefully, then keep the final version with your order. Start early enough to review a sample and allow time for packing and delivery before your celebration.
