How to choose a dry fruit gift box: jars, assortment and presentation

Compare jar counts, check food details and choose packaging that suits a personal or corporate gift.

By Hamperia Solutions · Updated October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Choose a jar count that fits the occasion and recipient.
  • Ask for net weight, assortment, ingredients and storage details.
  • Confirm packaging and delivery for the actual destination.

Choose the size for the occasion

Jar count is a useful first comparison, but it does not tell the whole story. Consider how many people will share the gift, whether it is a small thank-you or a larger festive gesture, and how the recipient will store it. A compact box may be easier to distribute to a team, while a larger assortment can suit a family or a more significant occasion.

Hamperia’s current catalogue lists a two-glass-jar box at ₹699, a three-jar box at ₹1,099 and a four-jar box at ₹1,499. Check the live listing and ask for the selected assortment, jar capacity, net weights and availability before placing an order.

Look beyond the jar count

Ask which dry fruits are included, whether the selection is fixed and how each item is packed. The number of jars does not indicate the quantity of food inside each jar. Clear net-weight information helps you compare options fairly and tell recipients what they are receiving.

Check the ingredient list, packing date, best-before information and storage instructions. Nuts are common allergens. If you are buying for a group, confirm whether the products may be packed or handled alongside other allergens and share accurate product information with recipients.

Choose a box that protects the contents

Glass jars need a box insert or cushioning that prevents them from knocking together during handling. Ask how the box will be packed and whether it is suitable for courier delivery to your destination. For a corporate event, confirm how many boxes fit in an outer carton and how the labels will be protected.

A coordinated ribbon or message card can personalise the gift without obscuring product labels. If adding custom branding, approve a proof and confirm whether it changes the packaging, minimum quantity or delivery schedule.

Confirm food and delivery details before ordering

Request a written contents list, final price and delivery estimate. Confirm whether the listed amount includes the gift box, personalisation and shipping. For a bulk order, give the recipient count and destination breakdown so packing and transport can be planned accurately.

Food contents and sourcing can change. Treat catalogue images as presentation references and confirm the exact assortment and labels for your order. Do not describe the gift as suitable for a dietary requirement unless every included product and its handling information has been checked.
