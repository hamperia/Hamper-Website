A practical corporate gifting checklist

A clear brief for employee, client and festive gifting enquiries.

By Hamperia Solutions · Practical gifting guide

Define the occasion and recipients

Separate employee recognition, client appreciation and festive gifting needs. Check recipient policies and preferences before choosing contents. Agree how many variants you can realistically manage.

Prepare a useful enquiry

Share quantity, budget per unit, date, destination and branding requirements. Include any ingredient restrictions or packaging preferences. An exact brief makes it easier to compare proposals fairly.

Approve contents and artwork

Check names, spelling, logo placement and message text. Confirm what any sample represents and whether the final items can vary. Agree replacements in writing if a particular product becomes unavailable.

Plan distribution

Confirm who receives the shipment and how gifts will be handed out. For multiple addresses, agree a secure way to share delivery details. Allow time to check quantities and packaging before the event.
