How to plan a bulk gift order for your team or clients

Prepare quantities, budget, personalisation and delivery details for a clear corporate gifting quotation.

By Hamperia Solutions · Practical gifting guide

Write a short gifting brief

List the occasion, recipient count, budget per gift and required date. Explain whether you need ready-made hampers, branded merchandise or individual components to assemble yourself.

Choose a manageable number of options

A small set of gift choices makes approval and packing easier. Separate employee and client options only when their needs differ. Confirm dietary restrictions and any organisation gift policies early.

Compare the complete quotation

Check the agreed contents, quantity, packaging, branding and delivery charges. Listed individual product prices do not settle the cost of a customised bulk order. Ask for the complete total and the payment terms before confirming.

Approve and keep a record

Review the final item list, artwork and any agreed sample. Record approved substitutions and delivery arrangements. Share recipient addresses only through the agreed order process and check the shipment against the order when it arrives.
