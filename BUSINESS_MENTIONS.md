# Hamperia: earning business mentions

No accounts, listings or outreach messages have been submitted as part of this update.

## Start with real relationships

1. Add https://hamperiasolutions.com/ to existing Hamperia business social profiles, using the same business name and contact email.
2. Ask a supplier you actually work with whether their stockist or partner page can mention Hamperia. Use the homepage or a relevant collection link.
3. After a completed corporate order, ask the customer whether they would like to share an onboarding or gifting story and credit Hamperia. Obtain permission before publishing their logo, photographs or order details.
4. Offer an original hamper-making demonstration or useful gifting guide to a relevant community, event organiser or publication. Let the publisher decide whether the material is useful and worth linking to.
5. Use a business association directory only if Hamperia really belongs to that association. Keep business details accurate and review listings periodically.

## Google Business Profile eligibility

Only pursue a Google Business Profile if Hamperia meets Google's in-person customer-contact requirements. Online-only businesses are not eligible; do not invent a shop address or customer-facing location.
Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036

Hamperia Solutions
contact@hamperiasolutions.com

## Track outcomes

Record the business/publication, existing relationship, relevant page, date contacted, response and published mention URL. Start with a small number of relevant contacts. Measure referral enquiries and useful coverage rather than the raw number of backlinks.

Avoid purchased ranking links, automated directory submissions and mandatory link exchanges. Paid promotions should be disclosed and their links appropriately marked by the publisher (for example, rel="sponsored").
Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam
